Try Prompt Privacy Policy

1. No Registration or Account Needed

The Try Prompt app is completely free to use. No accounts, login, or registration are required. We do not collect or request any personal identifiers (like names, emails, or phone numbers).

2. Local Storage

Your saved favorites and copy history log are stored locally on your device's storage. This data is never uploaded, synced, or shared with our servers or any third parties. Uninstalling the app will permanently delete all local data.

3. Cloud Database Connection

The app connects to Firebase Firestore solely to fetch categories and prompt templates. This connection is read-only. No user-specific data, logs, or selections are written to Firestore.

4. External Services

When sharing prompts, the app invokes your device's native share feature. External web links or developer email connections are opened in your default browser or mail client outside the app.
